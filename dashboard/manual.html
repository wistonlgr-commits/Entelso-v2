    CURRENTLY VIEWING: WAREHOUSE ADMINISTRATOR (WEB DASHBOARD) MANUAL
    You are reading standard operating procedures for desktop inventory management, batch imports, and compliance auditing.

    1. Executive KPI Monitoring & Live Analytics

    Upon signing into the web dashboard, warehouse supervisors receive an immediate overview of global equipment health via real-time Key Performance Indicators (KPIs). The top metrics banner aggregates total active fleet count, field deployment ratios, available stock ready for dispatch, and critical compliance alerts across all operational depots.

    2. Inventory Grid & Multi-Parameter Filtering

    The central inventory table organizes all instruments and tools into structured columns. Utilize the top search bar and category dropdowns to filter assets instantaneously by operational status (Available, In Use, Under Maintenance, or Quarantined), assigned engineering personnel, or geographical zone without page reloads.

    3. Bulk Multi-Asset Import & Embedded Excel Photo Extraction

    Entelso features an advanced spreadsheet ingestion engine capable of processing enterprise spreadsheets (such as Equipment Register.xlsx). Unlike traditional systems that strip out visual elements, Entelso extracts physical photographs embedded as floating drawing objects across columns (e.g., Photo 1, Photo 2).

    Automated Ingestion Workflow: When a spreadsheet is imported, the system intelligently correlates the row anchors of floating images with the equipment serial numbers (e.g., EQ-01 through EQ-29). Photographs are compressed, uploaded to secure cloud storage (Supabase entelso-media bucket), and permanently bound to the asset database records.

    Pro-Tip — Excel Photo Alignment: When preparing spreadsheets for ingestion, ensure that the top-left anchor of each photograph sits inside its corresponding equipment row. If an asset row has no embedded image in Excel, its photo gallery can be easily populated later from the dashboard or via WhatsApp uploads.

    4. Asset Detailed Audit Drawer & Specification Management

    Clicking any equipment row in the main table opens the Detailed Audit Drawer on the right-hand side of the screen. This inspection panel displays complete hardware specifications, brand/model numbers, warranty expiration dates, current custodian assignment, and high-resolution photo galleries.

    5. Alert Management & Expiration Tracking

    Safety compliance is strictly enforced across fall-protection equipment (harnesses, lanyards) and RF testing instruments. The system dynamically monitors calibration schedules and NFC tag inspection dates, categorizing assets into visual color-coded tiers:

    • 🔴 Critical / Expired (Calibración / Tag Vencido): Mandatory safety certifications have expired. The asset is highlighted in red and must be immediately withdrawn from field deployment.
    • 🟡 Warning / Due Soon (Próximo a Vencer): Equipment approaching inspection limits within 30 to 60 days, prompting supervisors to schedule preventive maintenance.
    • 🟢 Compliant / Certified: Fully calibrated instruments approved for field assignment.

    6. Real-Time Telemetry & Immutable Audit History

    Inside the Audit Drawer, switch to the History tab to review the immutable chronological audit trail. Every operation performed on an asset—whether checked out via WhatsApp by a field technician, transferred between zones, or edited by an admin—is recorded with an exact timestamp, location coordinates, and user identity verification.

    7. Bulk Multi-Asset Operations & Batch Reassignment

    When reorganizing depots or conducting quarterly audits, use the Bulk Multi-Asset Operations toolbar. Select multiple checkboxes on the left side of the table rows to display the floating batch action bar. From here, you can reassign dozens of tools to a new zone simultaneously or batch-generate QR labels.

    8. Automated Export & Regulatory Compliance Reporting

    To fulfill internal safety governance or external regulatory audits (ISO 9001, OSHA), click the Export Data button in the top toolbar. You can instantly download structured spreadsheets (.xlsx), CSV files, or PDF summaries containing calibration schedules and inspection compliance certificates.

    Compliance Notice: Any equipment showing an expired calibration date must be withdrawn from field deployment until recertified by an authorized quality control engineer.

    1. Instant Field Operations via WhatsApp Assistant

    Field technicians do not require specialized applications or VPN installations to interact with the Entelso inventory system. The official Entelso WhatsApp Assistant serves as an intelligent, real-time bridge to the core database, allowing engineers to verify equipment safety, report locations, and transfer custody in seconds directly from their mobile phones.

    2. WhatsApp Command Cheatsheet Table

    To interact with the assistant, send formatted commands directly to the Entelso WhatsApp bot number. The system recognizes both English and Spanish terminology:

    3. Geolocation Verification & Zone Tracking

    When operating across expansive client facilities or remote cell towers, maintaining geographical traceability is essential. Technicians can share their live location directly through WhatsApp to geo-tag equipment deployments:

    • Step 1: Query or check out the equipment via WhatsApp (e.g., send CHECKOUT EQ-09).
    • Step 2: Tap the attachment icon (📎) in WhatsApp and select Location → Send Your Current Location.
    • Step 3: The system binds the GPS coordinates to the asset's active telemetry log, allowing supervisors to visualize field distribution across operational zones.

    4. Field Asset Custody Transfer & Checkout

    To maintain strict accountability, every piece of hardware must be assigned to an active custodian. When you send a checkout command, the assistant initiates a secure custody handover protocol. Once authenticated, your full name and contact number are linked to the asset until you formally check it back in or transfer it to another engineer.

    5. Security PIN & 2-Factor Authentication

    To prevent unauthorized asset transfers, sensitive operations require personal authentication. Every authorized field engineer is assigned a unique 4-digit Security PIN and a linked WhatsApp phone number:

    • PIN Verification: When attempting to transfer high-value instruments or report critical damage, the assistant will prompt: 'Please enter your 4-digit security PIN to confirm.'
    • Account Identity Binding: Commands are strictly validated against the sender's registered WhatsApp phone number. If an unrecognized phone number attempts to query inventory, access is denied and flagged for audit review.

    6. QR Code & NFC Tag Scanning

    Physical tools are affixed with durable, industrial-grade QR code labels and NFC smart chips. Instead of typing serial numbers manually into WhatsApp, technicians can simply scan the QR sticker or tap their NFC-enabled smartphone against the equipment tag. This automatically triggers WhatsApp with pre-filled inventory queries for instant retrieval.

    7. Maintenance Incident Logging & Photo Attachments

    If an instrument is damaged in transit or fails field calibration, technicians can document the incident immediately via WhatsApp. Send REPORT EQ-15 damaged connector, then attach a physical photo of the damage to the chat. The system stores the photo in cloud storage, changes the asset status to Under Maintenance, and sends an urgent push alert to the warehouse supervisor.
