    Operations Guide

    Select your operational role below to review workflows tailored to your daily tasks. Field Technicians utilize WhatsApp for instant on-site tracking, while Warehouse Managers access the web dashboard for inventory administration.

    Field Operations via WhatsApp Bot

    1. Instant Asset Query & Status Lookup

    As a field technician, you do not need to install specialized software. To verify the real-time operational status, location, or assigned custodian of any tool, send its serial number or inventory code directly to the official Entelso WhatsApp Bot.

    Tip: You can type conversational sentences such as "Where is asset SN-2026-8819?" or simply send the identifier. The bot automatically identifies the equipment.

    2. Geolocation Verification & Zone Tracking

    When interacting with the bot, the system checks your assigned regional operational depot (such as NSW - Main Warehouse or VIC - Field Operations). It confirms whether the asset's registered zone matches your physical work area, immediately alerting you if equipment is outside its designated sector.

    3. Field Asset Custody Transfer

    To transfer equipment custody from your depot to another technician or zone, execute an instant transfer command. Send "Transfer asset [ID] to [Zone Name], PIN [123456]". The bot validates your security PIN and updates the central inventory database in real time without requiring paperwork.

    4. Maintenance Alert Reporting & Incident Logging

    If an instrument breaks down, suffers damage, or fails calibration check in the field, report it immediately via WhatsApp. Describe the fault along with the asset number. The bot changes the asset status to Under Maintenance and transmits a notification to warehouse administrators on their dashboard.

    5. QR Code & NFC Tag Scanning

    Company tools and measuring equipment are equipped with industrial-grade QR codes and NFC stickers. Using your standard smartphone camera, scan the sticker on any asset. WhatsApp opens automatically with the exact asset identifier pre-loaded, allowing fast check-in and check-out.

    6. Technician Security PIN Verification

    To maintain strict corporate accountability, state-changing commands (such as transfers, maintenance reporting, or decommissioning) require your 6-digit personal security PIN. All transactions are digitally signed and recorded in the audit log under your technician ID.

    Web Dashboard & Administration

    1. Executive KPI Monitoring & Live Analytics

    Upon signing into the web dashboard, administrators receive a comprehensive overview of enterprise assets via live Key Performance Indicators (KPIs). The top banner calculates total inventory valuation, operational efficiency rate, active maintenance tickets, and calibration compliance across all depots.

    2. Inventory Grid & Multi-Parameter Filtering

    The central inventory grid displays all equipment in clean, structured columns. Utilize the top search bar and category dropdowns to filter equipment instantly by status (Available, In Use, Under Maintenance), assigned engineering team, or geographical zone without page reloads.

    3. Asset Detailed Audit Drawer & Specification Management

    Clicking any equipment row in the table opens the Detailed Audit Drawer on the right-hand side of the screen. This panel provides complete hardware specifications, serial numbers, acquisition dates, warranty expiration timelines, current custodian details, and high-resolution photography.

    4. Real-Time Telemetry & Immutable Audit History

    Inside the Audit Drawer, switch to the History tab to review the immutable audit trail. Every action performed on an asset—whether checked out via WhatsApp by a field technician or edited by a warehouse supervisor—is recorded with an exact timestamp, location data, and user verification.

    5. Bulk Multi-Asset Operations & Batch Reassignment

    When reorganizing depots or conducting quarterly inventories, use the Bulk Multi-Asset Operations toolbar. Select multiple checkboxes on the left side of the table rows to display the floating batch action bar. From here, you can reassign dozens of tools to a new zone simultaneously or batch-print QR labels.

    6. Automated Export & Regulatory Compliance Reporting

    To fulfill internal governance requirements or external safety audits (ISO 9001, OSHA), click the Export Data button in the top toolbar. You can instantly download structured spreadsheets (.xlsx), CSV files, or PDF summaries containing calibration schedules and inspection compliance certificates.

    Compliance Notice: Any equipment showing an expired calibration date must be withdrawn from field deployment until recertified by an authorized quality control engineer.
